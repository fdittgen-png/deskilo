DesKiloBeta

A booking and money app for coworking communities.

Start

Explore the demo Sign in or get started Join with an invitation

The demo opens from the sign-in screen ("Explore the demo") and resets when you leave it. To join, sign in, then paste the code you were given. Nothing is created or joined by opening this page.

Running your own server is an operator's task, set up from the app's Server screen; it is never needed to try the app or join a space.

The three questions it answers

  • Where can I work? The plan shows every place and who holds it, for any day.
  • What do I owe? Each member reads their own statement: subscription, use, credits and payments.
  • Who must approve this? Expenses, deletions and changes go to the people the community named.

Try it in the demo

  1. Book a place for a half-day, then read what it used of your allowance on your statement.
  2. Submit a community expense and approve it as another member.
  3. Open a request still waiting for a decision and see who may decide it.

The demo is a fictional community on your device; nothing you do there reaches a server.

Who is responsible for what

  • The workspace owner sets the rules, prices and members.
  • A database administrator decides which assistants may connect and how.
  • Whoever runs the server keeps it updated and restorable.
  • Each member decides whether an assistant may act for them.

There is no support contract or guaranteed response time.

Plan your operating cost

Every value is yours to enter; nothing is looked up. A blank field is unknown, not free. Taxes are not included.
