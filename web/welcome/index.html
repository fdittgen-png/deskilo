Three questions, answered at a glance

Everyone sees the same truth.

Most tools are built for the person who runs the space. DesKilo is built for the community: the member and the treasurer read the same plan, the same figures and the same history.

Where can I work?

A live floor plan shows every desk and who holds it, for any day. Book a half-day, a desk or a whole room, once or as a series, then check in at the door.

What do I owe?

One transparent account per member: subscription, extra use, shared expenses and payments — with the same figures for the member and for whoever keeps the books.

Who decides?

Roles and approval rules your community chooses. Whatever an admin does on your behalf waits for your confirmation, and every decision keeps its history.

A tour in eight screens

From the first booking to the last invoice.

Swipe through the screens

  1. 01

    See who is where, for any day

    The plan shows each level, office and desk with its state and its occupant. Switch to a day, week or month view when you plan ahead.

  2. 02

    Book in two taps — or for someone else

    Morning, afternoon, a whole day or exact hours; once or every week. The sheet tells you upfront how the booking counts against the member's plan.

  3. 03

    Join with a code, check in with a badge

    Members join by scanning the space's QR code. Printed desk codes, NFC badges and a kiosk mode for a wall tablet handle walk-ins and check-in at the door.

  4. 04

    A fair account, readable by the member

    Plans, allowances, day packages and overage rules turn into one monthly statement: what was used, what is included, what is due — no surprises at the end of the month.

  5. 05

    Invoices that look right

    Gapless numbering, credit notes, VAT, reminders and a PDF in your own layout. Payments are recorded or handed off online, and the books export for your accountant.

  6. 06

    Decisions by the people you named

    Extra days, expenses, deletions, new members: each kind of request goes to the people your rules name, one after another if you wish — with a trail of who decided what.

  7. 07

    Talk without leaving the app

    Private conversations and groups that can point at a booking or an invoice. You choose who sees your name, contact details and presence — and the space operator never reads your messages.

  8. 08

    Draw your space in minutes

    Levels, rooms, desks and seats on a grid or over a photo of your floor. Each place carries its accessories and its NFC tag; rooms and levels can be booked as a whole.

Shared server or your own — or both

Your data lives where you decide.

Any DesKilo server can be linked to the global directory, so its public spaces are found next to everyone else's. One personal account connects them all.

The reference server

Create an account and a space in minutes. Hosted in the EU on the reference deployment run by the author, which also carries the global directory. Free for every space registered by 31 December 2027; spaces registered from 1 January 2028 pay a hosting fee.

Your own instance

Open a free account at supabase.com and choose “Create a new instance” in the app: with one access token it creates the project, installs the database, deploys the server functions and sets up sign-in — no terminal. Your workspaces stay there.

Both at once

Belong to a space on the reference server and to another on your association's server: Home lists them together and shows which server hosts each one.

Questions

Before you start

Is it really free?

The software is, and it stays free: no licence fee, no per-member price. Hosting is another matter. Hosting by the author, the reference server included, is free for every space registered by 31 December 2027; spaces registered from 1 January 2028 pay for hosting. Your own hosting, payment providers or other external services may cost something.

Where is our data stored?

On the server you choose. The reference deployment is hosted in the EU, with no tracking and no advertising. A self-hosted instance keeps its workspaces, members and conversations on that instance only.

Do members have to install anything?

No. The web app works in any browser. The Android and iPhone apps add push notifications and scanning QR codes with the camera.

How do I run my own server?

Open a free account at supabase.com, make a personal access token and choose “Create a new instance” on the app's server screen. The wizard creates the project, installs the schema, deploys the server functions and sets up sign-in. The technical guide covers backups and upgrades.

How do people find our space?

Publish a public page in the shared directory — an explicit owner action you can withdraw. A self-hosted server can be linked to the global directory too, so its public spaces are listed beside the others.

Is it ready for my space?

DesKilo is in beta and used by its first community. It suits spaces willing to run a pilot, check their own workflows and tell us what got in the way. Online payments and e-invoicing should be tested with your own provider first.

Can we change it or contribute?

Yes. The code is AGPL-3.0: keep the author's credit and publish your changes, or ask for a commercial licence. Translations, tests, bug reports and pull requests are welcome on GitHub.

Can DesKilo be adapted to our space?

Yes. Customisations can be commissioned from the author, including exclusive features built for your space only. Get in touch on GitHub.
