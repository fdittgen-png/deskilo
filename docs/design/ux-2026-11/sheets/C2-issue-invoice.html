What changes and why

  • Observed At desk width the issue form is a bottom sheet (08), unlike the other sales tasks that use the task surface.
  • Observed The preview’s three figures add up to the balance (150,00 + 16,00 − 50,40 = 115,60) and the button gives no amount and no word about the issued document (09).
  • Decided Desk: a side task. Order: recipient and month, then the preview (charges, tax, payments applied, balance — as the server computes them), then optional settings shown only where they apply. Phone: the same content in a sheet.
  • Decided The total is the balance as today (payments netted); no arithmetic changes.
  • Decided Footer: one line “The issued invoice is kept as an original and cannot be changed. It is sent by e-mail to bruno@atelier.test.” and the button “Issue invoice · 115,60 EUR”; disabled while the preview loads or has failed.
  • Decided States: preview loading, refusal said in words, uncertain outcome (“check the invoice register before trying again”), and a persistent success with a link to the original.
