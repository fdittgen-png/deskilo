What changes and why

  • Observed On a phone the six pending decisions come before the calendar’s own controls and their text ends in “…” (27).
  • Observed At the desk the Agenda mode draws two selectors for one page, and the top bar carries no title for the alerts view (31).
  • Decided Phone first view: the view control, the date, a “Filters” chip, then one row “Decisions (6)” that opens Alerts, then the agenda. With no decisions there is no row.
  • Decided In Alerts a decision’s text wraps; Accept and Decline are 48 dp and keep today’s permissions and steps.
  • Decided Desk: Alerts is a mode of the one “As” control with its count; the top bar names the page “Calendar” on every view.
