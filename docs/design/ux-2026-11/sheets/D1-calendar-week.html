What changes and why

  • Observed The pane’s one button always promises edit, cancel and show (03), while the sheet behind it offers only “Show on plan” for a booking the reader does not own (04).
  • Observed With the pane open the week is cut after Thursday (03); the location line can repeat a name (04).
  • Decided Desk header on one line: “Show” (Day / Week / Month), “As” (Grid / List / Agenda / Alerts 6), the date with previous, next and Today, the filters, “New booking” (primary), Export in the overflow.
  • Decided Own booking: Edit booking, Cancel booking, Show on plan as direct buttons. Someone else’s: only Show on plan. A second sheet only confirms a change or cancellation.
  • Decided The week keeps its seven columns with the pane open; where the window cannot hold both, the booking opens as a page. Each level is named once.
  • Hypothesis One “Filters · 2” control is enough at 1440; where the four dropdowns fit on the same line they may be shown instead. To be checked with a task walk.
