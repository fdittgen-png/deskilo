What changes and why

  • Observed The supplier-bill form is one ungrouped column of about eleven fields with no optional marks (11); the pinned actions are useful.
  • Decided Short choice: dialog at the desk, sheet on a phone. Record editor: side task at the desk. A long financial task such as this one: its own page on a phone, with a live review.
  • Decided Five numbered sections: 1 Supplier and document (searchable supplier, document number, document date) · 2 Amount and tax (net, tax rate, tax, total; currency shown as a fact) · 3 Payment (not paid / paid on, due date) · 4 Accounting (category, site) · 5 Attachment (the receipt).
  • Decided Sticky footer: Cancel, “Save purchase”. An error summary at the top links to the field; the variant shows “amount is not a number”.
  • Decided A value that cannot be edited is text with its reason beside it, not a disabled field; the currency is a fact here.
  • Hypothesis Five sections are quicker to scan than the flat list; to be checked with an unassisted task of entering a supplier bill and recovering from an invalid amount.
