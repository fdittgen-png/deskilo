768×844: the same compact summary in one row, To do collapsed to its count with “Open”, and the three customers inside the first view. Amounts of several currencies would sit side by side per currency, never added.

What changes and why

  • Observed Six of eight to-do kinds end in the same “View” button (07); the April row shows “-30,00” with no word for it.
  • Observed At 390 and 768 px (28, 29) the debt card and the to-do card are stacked at full height and the first customer starts below the first view.
  • Decided Desk: summary line “Amount due · Overdue · To review”, then the To do with task-named buttons (“Review April”, “Share reminders”, “Match payments”), then the customers table inside the first view.
  • Decided Phone and 768: the summary is one compact block of three figures in a row, per currency; To do is collapsed to its count with “Open”; the first customer rows are inside the first view.
  • Decided A negative amount is named for what it is (“credit 30,00 EUR”) or left out. “New invoice” keeps its word wherever it fits.
  • Hypothesis A collapsed To do on the phone is still noticed as work waiting; to be checked with a task walk.
