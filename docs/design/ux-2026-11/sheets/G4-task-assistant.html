What changes and why

  • Observed The one action that starts something sits between two large empty states (26).
  • Decided “Start a guide” leads, with its start buttons; an empty “My guides” or “Recordings” is one muted line.
  • Decided The page is drawn inside the shell of the business it was opened from (sheet A2).
  • Hypothesis Moving “Record a task” into the header makes it easier to find than the empty-state button; to be checked with a task walk.
