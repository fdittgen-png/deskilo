  • Decided The head says both counts by name: “This goal: 9 of 14 checks done” and “All steps: 12 of 29”; the current step’s name sits beside its number.
  • Decided The Demo explanation is a collapsed row below the steps, not the lower half of the page.
  • Decided A locked field (country, currency on step 1) stays a field with its reason next to it; nothing changes there.
