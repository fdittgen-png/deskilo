What changes and why

  • Observed Documents (19) creates through a floating plus, unlike the labelled buttons of other records pages, and shows a delete icon on every row.
  • Decided Order on every records page: header, one compact summary line (optional), search with a visible label plus status chips plus “Clear”, the table, then the result count and totals per currency.
  • Decided Table: identity, date, amount (right-aligned, tabular figures), currency, status, one row menu. Here: name, group, audience, added. The audience is shown on every row as text plus a symbol; deletion sits inside the row menu and still confirms.
  • Decided “Add document” is a labelled button in the page header. The least needed column gives way first (done in #4035).
  • Decided Desk: list and detail side by side when the list keeps its minimum, otherwise the record opens as a page. Phone: the same facts as rows, detail on its own page, a sticky action area.
  • Hypothesis The summary line helps for invoices and purchases but may be unneeded for documents; to be checked with a task walk.
