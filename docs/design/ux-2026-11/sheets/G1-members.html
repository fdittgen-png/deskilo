What changes and why

  • Observed The member page lists every future booking (14 in the Demo) before money and quick actions (06).
  • Observed The directory is a list of tall cards without a search field (05).
  • Decided Member page: identity and status with the role-appropriate actions first; “Next bookings” as three rows with “See all 14”; then money; access and roles apart from profile and payments.
  • Decided Directory: a search field with a label above the list; compact rows (name, role, status as text plus symbol) instead of tall cards.
  • Decided Role changes show their consequences before they apply and do not widen anyone’s authority.
  • Hypothesis Three rows are enough for the preview; to be checked with the task “find a member’s next booking”.
