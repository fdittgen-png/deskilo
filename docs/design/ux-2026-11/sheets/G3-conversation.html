What changes and why

  • Observed Bubbles take about 78 % of the window width, so a line of text runs far past a readable length (25).
  • Observed The composer is a field with a second row for attach and send; the top bar names nothing (25).
  • Decided The thread is a column of the reading measure (about 720 px); bubbles are no wider than the measure.
  • Decided Attach, field and send are one row at the desk and stay reachable above the keyboard on a phone.
  • Decided A conversation is in the shell of the business it is held in; the header names the person, and “Back to Me” stays in the sidebar. Where a conversation opens does not change.
  • Hypothesis The reading measure suits short chat messages as well as long ones; to be checked with a task walk.
