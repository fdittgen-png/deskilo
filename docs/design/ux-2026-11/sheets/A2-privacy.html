Privacy & data

Opened from the business sidebar: the same shell, the same sidebar, the breadcrumb names the page once. The in-app Back and the browser Back return to the page that was left (for example Documents) in its own shell.

What changes and why

  • Observed Opened from a business's sidebar, Privacy & data (20) and the task assistant (26) lose the sidebar and breadcrumb and look like another application.
  • Observed Capture 21 shows Documents in the narrow frame after Back from Privacy; this is not reproduced on the current tree and is the first thing the issue checks.
  • Decided Both pages are drawn inside the shell of the business they were opened from; opened from Me, the signed-out pages or a consent step they stay as today.
  • Decided The content keeps the reading measure (about 720 px) inside the shell instead of a separate frame.
  • Decided The task assistant is drawn in the same way (sheet G4).
