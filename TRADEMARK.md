# The DesKilo name and logo

**"DesKilo" and the DesKilo logo are trademarks of Florian DITTGEN.**
The [AGPL](LICENSE) gives you the code. It does not give you the name —
no free software licence does, and §7(e) of the AGPL says so explicitly.

## What you may do without asking

- Say your product is **built on DesKilo**, **compatible with DesKilo**,
  or **a fork of DesKilo**, as a statement of fact.
- Use the name in documentation, articles, talks, comparisons and
  academic work.
- Keep the name and the logo when you build DesKilo **unmodified** for
  your own use, or for a distribution such as F-Droid that builds from
  this repository.
- Use the name in the URL of a page *about* DesKilo.

## What needs permission

- Shipping a **modified** DesKilo under the name DesKilo, or under a
  name or logo close enough to be confused with it.
- Using the name or logo as the name of your company, product, service
  or app listing.
- Registering a domain whose main element is "deskilo", or a social
  account that reads as official.
- Implying endorsement, partnership or certification.

## Why

A fork is welcome — that is what the licence is for. But a person who
installs something called DesKilo should get the thing this project
stands behind. If a modified build carries the name and misbehaves, the
complaint arrives here, and neither we nor the person can tell the
difference. **Rename your fork and everything else in the licence still
applies to you**, including the right to sell what you build.

To ask for permission, write to the address in the repository's GitHub
profile.
