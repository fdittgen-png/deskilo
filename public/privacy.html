DesKilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Contact: fdittgen@gmail.com

DesKilo is a booking, community and ledger app for small self-organized spaces, published by Florian Dittgen (Germany). This page says, for each kind of data, what flows, to whom, when, and who decides. DesKilo is free software (AGPL-3.0-or-later): everything below can be checked in the code at https://github.com/fdittgen-png/deskilo.

Who is responsible

  • The space's owner runs each space — your community. The owner decides who is a member, the prices, the features the space uses and its payment providers, and answers for what you do in that space.
  • The server's operator runs the server a space lives on and stores its data. The app's default, the shared reference server, is operated by Florian Dittgen on Supabase in the EU (eu-central-1). A community can run its own server instead; then whoever runs it is the operator, and the data is wherever that operator put it.
  • The app's publisher, Florian Dittgen, writes the app. The app contains no tracking or analytics and sends nothing to its publisher.

In the app, Settings → Privacy & data shows the notice of the server you use — who runs it, the region, and each recipient with its purpose and transfer safeguard — and the space's own notice where it has one. Where the operator has not recorded something, the notice says so rather than guessing.

Messages

  • Only participants open a conversation: the two of you, the members of a group or, for a message to a space, the hosts named before you write. A space's owners and admins cannot read your conversations; the server enforces this, it is not a setting.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted. A message is stored on the server the conversation lives on, and whoever runs that server can technically reach its database. This is an access rule, not encryption — one more reason a community may prefer to run its own server.
  • Across spaces and servers. Your conversations from every space and every server you connected appear in one list. Writing to someone on another server, or asking to join a space there, connects you to that server first and asks you before anything is sent; that conversation is then stored on that server, under its operator.

Push notifications

Push is an outbound flow, and optional. It works only where the server's operator has set it up and the space uses it. When it is on for a device, that device's address and each notification go to the push service (Firebase Cloud Messaging, which passes notifications for Apple devices on to Apple). A notification carries a generic text only; the details are read in the app. Switch it off in Settings → Privacy & data → Push notifications on this device: the app keeps working and nothing more is sent to that device. The installed app can also remind you of a booking on the device itself, without any push service. A build prepared for F-Droid carries no push transport and no Google services at all. Whether it can be installed from F-Droid yet is stated in one place, the F-Droid status.

What we do NOT do

  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising. The app contains no third-party tracking or analytics SDK and no ad SDK. Firebase is used only to deliver push notifications, where the operator set it up.
  • No sale of data. Data reaches only the recipients named above and in the server's and the space's notices.
  • No payment card data. Card and wallet details are entered on the payment provider's own pages; DesKilo only records that a payment happened and its amount.

Retention & deletion

  • Leaving one space. Your open bookings there are cancelled, the messages you sent in it are deleted, and your membership is marked exited; it keeps no personal data of its own.
  • Leaving your last space. Your profile is cleared as well: name, photo, WhatsApp number, status, address and VAT id.
  • Accounting records stay. Ledger entries and invoices are the space's books: they are kept for the statutory accounting period of the space's country (ten years in France and Germany), and invoices stay exactly as issued.
  • Badges can be revoked at any time; a revoked hash stops working immediately.

The full retention table is in PRIVACY.md.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, export and erase your personal data, to restrict its use and to object. Export, erasure and rights requests are buttons in Settings → Privacy & data; a request to a space is answered within one calendar month. For anything else, contact fdittgen@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

Children

DesKilo is a workplace tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

In-app consent

Every account accepts this policy inside the app before using it (version 2026-10-10; the accepted version and date are stored on the account). A change of the text asks for acceptance again. The same text is readable anytime in Settings → Privacy & data → Your data, your rights, in the in-app help (Privacy) and in the online user guide.
