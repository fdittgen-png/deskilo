DesKilo manages a small coworking space: the floor plan and desk bookings,
the members, the shared ledger and the monthly invoices.

A REVIEWER NEEDS AN ACCOUNT AND A WORKSPACE. Two ways in:

1. Use the demo credentials in the fields above. They belong to a workspace
   that already has a floor plan, members, a ledger and issued invoices, so
   every tab has something to show.

2. Or sign up in the app and create a workspace: "Create an account" on the
   sign-in screen, then "Create a workspace" — the editor opens on an empty
   grid where a level, a desk and a seat can be drawn in under a minute.
   A brand-new workspace is empty by design; the money and invoice tabs
   only fill once bookings exist.

The backend is Supabase (EU, eu-central-1). No account data is shared with
third parties and the app contains no tracking, analytics or advertising
SDK. Online payments, when a workspace enables them, are handled on the
provider's own pages (PayPal, Stripe, Mollie, Wero) — the app only records
that a payment happened.
