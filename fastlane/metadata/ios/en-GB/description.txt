Your coworking space, run together. Book a desk on a live floor plan, give every member the same transparent account the admins see, and let the people your community names approve what matters. Free and open source (AGPL-3.0), in beta and ready for a pilot.

TRY IT BEFORE YOU INSTALL
• Demo in your browser: open www.deskilo.org and tap "Try the demo" — no account needed, and nothing you do there leaves your device
• User guide in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian, every screen explained step by step: www.deskilo.org/guide/en.html
• Setup guide for a small space, from the floor plan to the first invitation: www.deskilo.org/guide/setup-en.html

WHERE CAN I WORK?
• A live floor plan shows every desk and who holds it, for any day
• Book a half-day, a desk or a whole room, once or as a series, then check in at the door
• Join by scanning the space's QR code; printed desk codes, NFC badges and a kiosk mode for a wall tablet

WHAT DO I OWE?
• One transparent account per member: subscription, extra use, shared expenses and payments
• Plans, allowances, day packages and overage rules become one monthly statement
• Invoices with gapless numbering, credit notes, reminders and VAT, issued in the app for spaces in France and Germany; accounting exports for your accountant

WHO DECIDES?
• Roles and approval rules your community chooses
• Whatever an admin does on your behalf waits for your confirmation, and every decision keeps its history

ACROSS SPACES
• One "Me" for every space on a server, with favourites and your own order
• Discover: a shared directory and map where spaces publish a public page on purpose
• Messenger: private conversations and groups; you choose who sees your name, contact details and presence

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Use the shared reference server, or host the backend yourself on your own Supabase. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising. Available on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS and in a browser, in five languages.

GDPR: EU-hosted data on the reference deployment, access limited by role and enforced on the server. Your rights are buttons in Settings → Privacy & data: who can see your data, who accessed it, export, erasure.

COST: the software is free, with every feature, also on your own server. Hosting on the reference deployment is free for everyone until 31 December 2027; from 1 January 2028 it stays free up to five active subscribers (eight for nonprofits) and is paid above that, from about €29 a month. Billing happens outside the app.
