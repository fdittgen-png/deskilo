DesKilo runs the whole life of a small, self-organised coworking space: the desks, who sits where, what each member owes, and the invoices at the end of the month. No spreadsheet, no group chat thread about who paid what.

THE SPACE
• Draw your space on a grid: levels, offices, desks, bookable seats
• Tap a seat on the live floor plan to check in — walk-ups can never double-book
• Reserve ahead, once or as a repeating series, and scroll time to see any moment of any day
• Book a whole room, or a whole level, when the space works that way
• Scan a space's QR code to book it from the door

THE COMMUNITY
• Members directory with who is in today
• Invitations by link or QR; join requests are confirmed, never silent
• Everything an admin does on your behalf needs your confirmation
• A wall tablet in kiosk mode checks people in by badge, QR or camera

THE MONEY
• Membership plans with half-day quotas; overage priced by a band you set
• One transparent ledger per member: subscription, usage, services, payments
• Community expenses: buy the coffee, get it credited on your next statement
• Day packs for occasional members
• Pay online through PayPal, Stripe, Mollie or Wero, if your space enables it

THE PAPERWORK
• Monthly invoices, derived from what actually happened — never typed by hand
• Signed and immutable: a mistake is corrected by a replacement, and both stay
• VAT, if you charge it: rates per service, the breakdown on every document
• EN 16931 e-invoices (UBL, CII and Factur-X) for business and public customers
• Accounting exports your accountant already reads: SAF-T, and the French FEC

EVERYWHERE
The same account on Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and in a browser. A desk booked on a phone shows up on the wall tablet a second later.

PRIVACY
No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK. The backend is Supabase in the EU, and you can host it yourself. DesKilo is open source under the 0BSD licence — everything above can be read in the code.
